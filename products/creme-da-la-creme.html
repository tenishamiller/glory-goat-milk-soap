Why You'll Love This Cream

Every ingredient has been carefully chosen for a reason.

Fresh Goat Milk

Fresh goat milk is naturally rich in vitamins, minerals, proteins, and moisturizing fats that help keep skin feeling hydrated, soft, and healthy-looking. It has been treasured in skincare for generations because of its naturally gentle and nourishing qualities.

Pumpkin Seed Oil

Pumpkin seed oil is full of essential fatty acids, antioxidants, vitamin E, and naturally occurring minerals that help replenish moisture and support healthy-looking skin. It leaves skin feeling conditioned, smooth, and refreshed.

Evening Primrose Oil

Evening primrose oil is one of my favorite skincare oils because it's naturally rich in gamma-linolenic acid (GLA), which helps support the skin's moisture balance. It leaves your skin feeling silky, calm, and beautifully cared for.

Shea Nilotica

Shea Nilotica is softer, creamier, and more luxurious than traditional shea butter. It melts beautifully into the skin, providing rich moisture while leaving behind a smooth, velvety finish without feeling overly greasy.

Ingredients

Fresh Goat Milk, Distilled Water, Evening Primrose Oil, Pumpkin Seed Oil, Shea Nilotica, Emulsifying Wax, Stearic Acid, Optiphen (Preservative), Fragrance Oil.
