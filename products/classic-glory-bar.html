The Classic Glory Bar (Unscented)

I decided to stop adding fragrance to my goat milk soap because I want the quality of the soap to be the main focus. I don't want the scent to be the reason you love it—I want you to love it because of how your skin feels after using it.

The Classic Glory Bar is made with goat milk and a blend of olive, coconut, and palm oils. It gives you a rich, creamy lather and cleans your skin without leaving it feeling dry and stripped.

It is simply a good, carefully made bar of soap that leaves your skin feeling clean, soft, and cared for. Made by loving, happy, and appreciative blessed hands—mine. Lol.
