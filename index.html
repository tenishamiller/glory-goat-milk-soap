The Difference

What's so special about goat milk soap?

Natural fats, vitamins, and lactic acid work together to nourish and gently renew with every wash. Simple, purposeful ingredients — handmade with care at Glory Goat Milk Soap.

About Glory

The works of my hand are indeed blessed—and I am referring to the scripture above this website. I would not have this business without God. I would not have this business without the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit.

I asked God for a way to make money by doing something that makes me happy and doesn't feel like work. God called me to be an entrepreneur ever since I was a very young child. I would not stop having ideas on how to start businesses and make money. This is how I knew I was called to be an entrepreneur—to be a businesswoman—which the Holy Spirit reminded me of through the scripture in Proverbs 31 that refers to Christian businesswomen and businessmen. Entrepreneurs.

She considers a field and buys it. Out of her earnings, she plants a vineyard. She sets about her work vigorously. Her arms are strong for her tasks. She sees that her trading is profitable, and her lamp does not go out at night.

Proverbs 31

This business did not come without great and mighty warfare. And I'm not just talking about one year or two years of warfare—I'm talking about warfare that started since I came out of the womb.

I have tried to start a business so many times. And because of God's grace and glory, they all failed. And I realize why they failed: it's because I needed to be trained. It's because God needed to do a great and mighty work on my heart. God removed my stony heart and He gave me a fleshly one—and that took time.

I have lost thousands upon thousands of dollars trying to start a business, and I realized that the reason why they all failed is because I did it without God. Without Him I can do nothing, because it is written that God is my firm foundation. So He saved me from building businesses on foundations made of sand or wood or clay, and helped me to build my foundation on a brick—brick by brick by brick.

As I was writing this, the enemy wanted me to not mention God on my website, saying that people would be turned away by it. But I refused. I refused to listen to the lies of the enemy. And I'm letting everyone who purchases from me know that my success comes from God, that my business ideas come from God, that my earnings come from God—and I give great and mighty glory to Him. Because if no one does it, I will! The very breath from my body came from Him, and I refuse to be ashamed of Him.

Thank you for supporting my ministry. Thank you for supporting the gifts that He puts in me. And I concur that the very works of my hands are blessed—and I ask and pray that you see that with each product that you purchase from me.

God bless you all.

♥ Tenisha
